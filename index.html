The Old Way

Race weekend ends. Then the work begins.

⚠

Files scattered across the SD card

VBOX HD2 splits a session across multiple files. Manually merging them is error-prone and tedious.

⚠

No lap detection in i2

Open a converted file in MoTeC and the whole session is one giant lap. No splits, no comparisons, no analysis.

⚠

Cryptic channel names

"nmot," "ath," "vwheel_fl"—not exactly intuitive. You spend an hour mapping them before you can build a single worksheet.

⚠

Hours per car, every weekend

Multiply the above by every car on the team, every session, every event. The data engineer drowns.

The New Way

Drop in, download, done.

The converter handles the parts that aren't worth your time, so you can spend the morning analyzing data instead of preparing it.

01

Auto track detection

GPS coordinates identify the track automatically. COTA, Sebring, Sonoma, VIR, Laguna Seca—every track on the GR Cup calendar, recognized on upload.

02

Lap detection that works

Reads the start/finish line from the VBOX HD2 itself. Beacon and Lap Number channels arrive properly populated, so i2 splits laps the moment you open the file.

03

All 73 channels, properly named

"Engine RPM" instead of "nmot." "Throttle Pos" instead of "ath." "Wheel Speed FL/FR/RL/RR" instead of "vwheel_*." Tuned for the Bosch DDU on the GR86.

04

Multi-file session merge

Drop a folder of files from a single session and get one continuous LD back, with lap timing preserved across the joins. Pro and Team tiers.

05

Organized output

Files come back named Q1_42_L1-L8.ld, organized into GRCup_COTA_2026-04-24/. Drop straight into your MoTeC workspace.

06

Your data isn't kept

Files are deleted automatically after download. We never train on, share, or retain your telemetry.
